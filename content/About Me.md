I am a science communicator and neuroscientist passionate about the intersection of science and society. I completed my PhD in Neuroscience at UNC Chapel Hill in 2026 and am now based in Copenhagen, Denmark. Guided by a belief in deeply collaborative and community-driven research, I love writing about topics in neuroscience, genetics, and social issues in accessible ways that highlight the reciprocal relationship between science and our societies.
